01 /

The feeling returns

I don’t know exactly why this music and this image move me, but they do. Together, they bring back the amazement I felt as a child looking up at the sky.

The children running remind me of being outside—in the fields or along the streets—finding something beautiful wherever I looked. The colours of nature, the clouds overhead, and the stars appearing as the sky grew dark. I didn’t need to know their names to stop and look.

There is one memory that comes back especially clearly.

02 /

A light I almost touched

When I was little, my father took me with him to work. At one point, a friend of his was carrying me. It was dark, and an aeroplane was passing overhead, its red light blinking against the night sky.

I stretched out my hands towards it.

Somehow, from where I was being held, it seemed close enough to reach. My father’s friend noticed what I was trying to do and gently lowered me, playfully teasing me. I began to cry. I thought I had been so close—just a little higher, and I could have touched the plane.

He was only playing with me. But to me, that small blinking light was something I had almost held in my hands.

Now, when I listen to Constellations and look at this image, I remember that feeling. Not just the sky, but what it was like to look at it before I understood the distances.
